Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account with Spendly, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. When you use the App, we also store the expense records you choose to enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide.

We do not collect payment information, government-issued identifiers, or any sensitive personal data beyond what is required to operate the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

The data you provide is used solely to deliver and improve the Spendly service. Specifically, we use it to:

— Display your expense history and spending summaries within your account.
— Authenticate you when you sign in.
— Respond to support requests or account-related queries.
— Improve the performance and reliability of the App over time.

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. Passwords are hashed and are never stored in plain text.

If you choose to delete your account, all associated data — including your expense records — will be permanently removed from our systems within a reasonable timeframe. This action is irreversible.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly may use trusted third-party services for infrastructure and analytics purposes (for example, hosting providers). These providers are bound by their own privacy policies and are not permitted to use your data for any purpose outside of providing services to us.

We do not integrate with any third-party advertising networks, data brokers, or social media tracking tools.

5. Cookies

Spendly uses session cookies strictly to keep you logged in during your visit. We do not use tracking cookies, third-party cookies, or any form of cross-site behavioural tracking.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the way we handle your data, please reach out to us via the contact information provided on our website. We aim to respond to all privacy-related enquiries within a reasonable time.
